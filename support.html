Support

We're here to help

Contact us in the app

Open GameChallenger and go to Profile → Support. Describe the issue with your email and mobile number, and our team will reply by email, normally within 24 hours.

Common questions

I didn't receive my verification code

Codes expire after 10 minutes. Check your Spam or Junk folder and search for noreply@we-challenge.net, or choose to receive the code by SMS instead.

How do I join a tournament?

Add your verified gaming ID for the tournament's platform in Profile → Gaming IDs, open the tournament and tap Join. The entry cost in Coins is shown before you confirm.

Can I buy Coins or cash them out?

No. Coins are only for tournament entry and prizes. They can't be purchased, transferred or exchanged for cash.

How do I report a player, a result or content?

Block a player from their profile at any time. To report a player, a disputed result or inappropriate content, contact us through Profile → Support with the details.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Premium is billed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → GameChallenger. You can restore a purchase from the Premium screen in the app.

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account and personal data inside the app:

  1. Open GameChallenger and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is closed immediately, you are signed out on all devices, and your profile is no longer visible to other players. Coins and progress cannot be recovered, and the same email address and mobile number cannot be used to register again. To have your remaining personal data erased, send a request through Support in the app and we will complete it within the period required by law. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.
