Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data GameChallenger ("we", "us") collects when you use the GameChallenger mobile app and related services, why we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. We process personal data in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations.

1. Who can use GameChallenger

GameChallenger is only for people aged 18 or over who live in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

2. Data we collect

CategoryWhat it includes
Account detailsUsername, email address, mobile number, password (stored only as a secure hash), date of birth, gender and nationality, and whether your email and phone are verified.
ProfileProfile photo (if you add one), Xbox and PlayStation gaming IDs, game interests, profile style, XP, level and achievements.

Sponsored content interactionsWhen you view or tap a sponsored banner or like or share a news article.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, including sending one-time verification codes by SMS or email.
  • To run tournaments: registration, brackets, results, prizes, refunds and the leaderboard.
  • To provide social features such as friends, chat, invitations and tournament updates.
  • To send notifications you can control in the app, such as match reminders, invitations and results.
  • To provide Premium features and confirm your subscription status.
  • To keep the platform fair and safe: preventing fraud, cheating, spam and abuse, enforcing our Terms, and keeping audit records of administrative actions.
  • To answer support requests and improve the app.

Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (the Terms of Use), our legitimate interest in running a safe and fair service, compliance with law, and your consent where we ask for it (for example, push notifications, which you can turn off at any time).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf to run the app:

  • Replit — application hosting, database and file storage (including profile photos).
  • Google Firebase — phone sign-in verification and push notifications.
  • Apple — App Store purchases and push notification delivery.
  • RevenueCat — managing Premium subscription status.
  • Twilio — sending SMS verification codes.
  • Resend — sending email verification codes and service emails.
  • OpenAI — generating optional AI tournament banners. Only the game, platform and tournament type are sent; no personal data.

Other players can see your public profile (username, photo, nationality, level, gaming achievements) and your participation in tournaments. Private tournaments are visible only to their host and invited or registered players. Some providers may process data outside Saudi Arabia; where they do, we rely on the safeguards the PDPL requires.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or the public.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, it is closed immediately, signed out on all devices and removed from the app for other players. To prevent abuse, the email address and mobile number of a deleted account cannot be used to register again. We keep the remaining account records only as long as needed for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes (such as wallet and tournament records). You can ask us at any time to erase the rest of your personal data; see "Your rights" below. Verification codes expire within minutes.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPL you can ask to be informed about how we process your data, access it, get a copy, correct it, and have it deleted, and you can withdraw consent at any time. You can edit most profile details in the app, deactivate your account, or delete it permanently from Profile → Account Settings → Delete account. For any other request, contact us as described below. You also have the right to complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

7. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived sign-in tokens stored in secure device storage, rate limiting, and restricted, audited administrative access. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

8. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will let you know in the app before they take effect. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: use Profile → Support in the app, or see our Support page.
